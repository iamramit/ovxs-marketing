### So what
- AI tools sent 693% more traffic to US retail sites over the 2025 holidays, and it converted 31% better than other traffic (Adobe).
- Many small stores may already be listed in AI shopping channels without choosing it. Check the store’s settings rather than assume.
- What wins in an AI answer is what sells on the store: clear product data and plain answers. That takes work, not a new tool.

## Angles
### A — Your shoppers are asking a chatbot first
- Hook: More of your shoppers now meet your products in a chat window before they ever see your store.
- Argument: AI answers draw on what your store already says, so clear product data and honest pages matter more right now than another redesign.
- Proof used: Adobe 693% rise and 31% better conversion; Shopify/ChatGPT changes in 2026.
- Fits: Founder POV · Everyone
- Risk: The details move fast. Keep the copy on principles, not on any one platform feature.

### B — What building Ovi taught me
- Hook: I built an AI assistant for our own site before I’d sell one to anyone.
- Argument: The hard part wasn’t the AI. It was writing down plain answers to real questions, the same work that makes a store sell.
- Proof used: Ovi article; AI assistant $1,500 setup + $199/month (only in the CTA, if at all).
- Fits: Founder POV · Everyone
- Risk: Can read as an advert for the AI assistant. Keep the offer to one line, and quote no Ovi volume numbers.

### C — Most small stores don’t need a chatbot yet
- Hook: Most small stores don’t need an AI chatbot. Yet.
- Argument: Fix product pages and answers first. A chatbot can only repeat what your store already says.
- Proof used: Adobe stats as context; a merchant’s report of 1–3 chats a day on 20,000 visitors (anecdotal, so “one merchant told the community”).
- Fits: Founder POV · Everyone
- Risk: Undercuts our own AI assistant offer. That honesty is the point, but Ramit needs to be comfortable with it.
