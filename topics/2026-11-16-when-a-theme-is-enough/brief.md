### What's already out there
- Theme vendors and agencies writing “free vs premium vs custom” comparisons, each with a stake in the answer. Common take: start with a theme, go custom later, with vague triggers.
- The gap: a studio that builds custom stores saying plainly when you don’t need one, with concrete signs that you’ve outgrown a theme.

### So what
- A new store can start on a free Shopify theme. Shopify released 10 new free ones in 2025, and paid themes run up to about $400 (Shopify, 2026).
- Custom design starts around $1,000 and climbs fast (Shopify, 2026). For a first store, that money usually does more as stock, photos and ads.
- Outgrowing a theme is a real thing with clear signs. Name them, and say plainly that our own Launch is built on a proven theme.

## Angles
### A — Our Launch starts with a theme. Yours can too.
- Hook: Most first stores don’t need a custom design. Ours start on a proven theme too.
- Argument: A good theme, set up around your products, sells as well as a custom build at a fraction of the cost; spend the difference on photos and stock.
- Proof used: Launch from $3,000 on a proven theme; Shopify theme and custom cost ranges (2026); Horizon free themes (2025).
- Fits: Honest advice · New founders
- Risk: Founders may hear “just buy a $400 theme and skip us”. Be fine with that, and show what setup work still matters.

### B — Five signs you’ve outgrown your theme
- Hook: You don’t need a custom Shopify store until your theme starts saying no.
- Argument: Stay on a theme until you hit specific limits — subscriptions, configurators, a catalogue it can’t show — and then fix only those, often with one custom section.
- Proof used: custom section from $250; Growth build from $6,500 for when it’s truly needed; Shopify cost ranges.
- Fits: Honest advice · New founders and growing stores
- Risk: A list post can feel generic. Each sign needs a concrete store example from our experience.

### C — Spend the custom-design money on photos
- Hook: If you have $10,000 for your first store, don’t spend it on design.
- Argument: Early sales come from product, photos and clear pages, so a founder’s first budget belongs there, with the store on a well set up theme.
- Proof used: Shopify cost ranges (custom $1,000–$150,000+, most agencies $1,000–$15,000); New to Shopify plan $247/month + $500 setup as the low-cost route.
- Fits: Honest advice · New founders
- Risk: The $10,000 figure is illustrative, not a stat. Present it as an example, and don’t give budget splits we can’t source.
