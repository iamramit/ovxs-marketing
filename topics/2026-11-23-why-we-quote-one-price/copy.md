## LinkedIn post

Two quotes for the same store: $1,000 and $15,000. Both can be honest.

Say both land in your inbox on the same morning. Same brief. Same store.

The low one covers a theme, set up and handed over.

The high one covers design, product pages, moving your data, testing and support after launch.

Neither studio is hiding anything. They priced different jobs.

The range is real. In a survey quoted by Shopify, 63% of agencies put custom work between $1,000 and $15,000.

So before you compare the totals, compare what’s in them:

→ Who writes and uploads the product pages?
→ Is moving products, customers and orders included?
→ Who checks redirects, so old links still work?
→ How many rounds of changes?
→ What happens after launch, and for how long?
→ What does something new cost, and who agrees it first?

If the answers match, the cheaper quote may be the right one. Take it.
