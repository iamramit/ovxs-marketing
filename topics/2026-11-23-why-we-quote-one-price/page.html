Say you get two quotes for the same store. One is $1,000. The other is $15,000. Both can be honest.

Shopify quotes a survey where 63% of agencies put custom work between those two figures. Often, the gap is scope.

Before you compare totals, ask each studio the same questions. Are product pages included? Is moving your data? How many rounds of changes? What happens after launch? What does a new request cost, and who agrees it first?

If the answers match, the cheaper quote may be the right one.

Our rule is plain. Once scope is agreed, every project gets one fixed price. New requests are quoted separately, never added silently.
