### What's already out there
- Top results are redirect-app vendors and migration agencies with long checklists, plus Shopify’s own guide. Common take: “map every URL, add 301s, submit a sitemap.” Several quote 20–40% traffic loss figures we couldn’t trace to a study.
- The gap: a calm, senior view. The platform isn’t what costs rankings; skipped redirects and changed content are. Plus a plain answer on timing (don’t move before your busy season) and what to check in the first 30 days.

### So what
- Moving platform doesn’t cost rankings by itself. Missing redirects, changed URLs and lost page content do, and Shopify says some short dip is normal.
- Changing your domain at the same time is the riskier move: one study of 892 domain migrations found an average of 523 days to recover traffic (SEJ, 2025). Keep the domain if you can.
- Timing is a free win. Move at least six months before your busy season, or right after it, and keep every redirect live for good.

## Angles
### A — The platform isn’t what costs you rankings
- Hook: Moving to Shopify won’t lose your Google rankings. Skipping the boring parts will.
- Argument: Rankings drop when old URLs break and page content changes, so the work that protects them is a full redirect map and keeping what already ranks.
- Proof used: Shopify SEO migration guide (some dip is normal, keep redirects a year+); Move to Shopify from $1,500 with every old URL redirected.
- Fits: Shopify know-how · Movers
- Risk: Can read as “nothing to worry about”. Keep the honest line that a short dip is normal.

### B — Move in your quiet season
- Hook: The best time to move to Shopify is the month after your busiest one.
- Argument: Some short-term traffic dip is normal, so timing the move away from peak sales is the cheapest protection a store has.
- Proof used: Shopify guide (six months before busy season, or right after it); redirect limits and rules from Shopify Help Center.
- Fits: Shopify know-how · Movers
- Risk: Publishing in late November, many stores are in peak now. Frame it as “plan it for January”, which fits the timing well.

### C — Change one thing at a time
- Hook: Moving platform, changing your domain and redesigning in the same week is three risks stacked on one launch.
- Argument: Each change can dent traffic on its own, so move first, keep the domain and content, and redesign once the numbers have settled.
- Proof used: SEJ study of 892 domain migrations (523 days average recovery, 17% not after 1,000 days); Shopify guide; Move from $1,500, new design from $3,000 as separate steps.
- Fits: Shopify know-how · Movers
- Risk: The SEJ numbers are about domain changes, not platform moves. The copy must say so plainly or it overstates the danger.
