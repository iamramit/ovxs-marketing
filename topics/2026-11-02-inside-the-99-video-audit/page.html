What’s inside a $99 video audit

Fifteen minutes, three fixes. A ranked list you can act on this month beats a complete list you can’t.

Say a store owner pays for an audit and gets 40 pages back. They read the first six. The rest sits in a folder.

Nothing in it is wrong. There’s just too much of it to act on. They have a store that isn’t selling enough, a long document explaining why, and no idea where to start on Monday morning.

That’s the problem with most store audits. This article explains what’s inside our $99 video audit, and why we built it to give you three fixes rather than every fix.

Store owners don’t lack checklists

You can find a checklist for almost anything on a Shopify store. Checkout, product pages, speed, navigation, trust signals. Most of them are fine. Few of them help.

Baymard’s checkout usability benchmark finds 32 possible improvements on the average benchmarked checkout. That’s one part of one store, at the level of the top sites they study.

Nobody fixes 32 things. A small team with a normal week doesn’t have the time, and a founder doesn’t have the budget. So the list gets filed, and nothing changes.

A long audit feels thorough. In practice, it hands the hard part back to you: deciding what matters first.

What you actually get

The video audit is a 10–15 minute recorded walkthrough of your public site. A senior person looks at your store, talks through it, and records the screen as they go.

Inside the video:

  • What’s working. So you know what not to break while you fix the rest.
  • What’s costing you sales. The specific places shoppers are likely to hesitate, get confused or leave.
  • Three fixes, ranked. In the order we’d do them, so you can start with the first one this month.

That’s all of it. No 40-page PDF, no scoring matrix, no appendix.

Why three, and why ranked

Ranking is the work. Anyone can produce a long list. Deciding which three things matter most for your store, your shoppers and your budget takes experience.

Some of the three will be small. A change you can make in an afternoon, or an app that does the job, and we’ll say so. Some may need a developer. We’ll tell you which is which, even when the honest answer means less work for us.

A short list also has a practical advantage. You can finish it. Three fixes done this month will do more for your sales than thirty fixes planned for next year.

Some lost sales aren’t fixable

This is the part most audits skip. Not every shopper who leaves was ever going to buy.

Baymard’s research on cart abandonment found that 42% of US online shoppers have abandoned a cart because they were just browsing or not ready to buy. No checkout change wins that visit. They were looking, and they left.

So part of the audit’s job is to separate the losses you can’t fix from the ones you can. We’d rather tell you a problem isn’t worth chasing than send you off to fix something that was never broken.

The sales worth winning back are the ones where a shopper wanted to buy and something got in the way. That’s what we look for.

How it works

Here are the terms, plainly:

  • $99. One fixed price.
  • No logins, no call. We only look at your public site, the same way a shopper would.
  • Delivered within 3 working days.
  • Full refund if your site isn’t a fit.
  • Credited in full against any project you start with us within 60 days.

If you’d rather talk it through, or the project is bigger, we also offer a free 30-minute intro call with senior people. No pitch, no obligation. But if you want a straight answer you can act on, the video is the quicker route.

Who it suits

The audit suits stores that look fine but don’t sell enough. You’ve put work into the design, the products are good, and traffic arrives. Sales just don’t match.

It also suits founders who’ve been burned by vague feedback. Ask for honest opinions about your store online and you mostly get vendors who can’t promise anything, or replies that sound confident without having looked closely. A senior walkthrough with a refund behind it is a clearer offer.

If your store needs a rebuild, we’ll say that too. If three small fixes will do the job, we’ll say that instead. If speed or conversion is the main problem, our Speed, SEO & Conversion service picks up from there.

Business first. Design second.

A better-looking store won’t fix a store that doesn’t sell. Find the three things that are costing you sales, fix them in order, then decide what else is worth the money.

That’s what fifteen minutes of honest attention can give you. Not everything that could be improved. The three things worth improving first.
