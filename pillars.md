# Content pillars

Every piece of content belongs to one pillar. Aim for a weekly mix: two Honest advice, one Proof, one Shopify know-how, and Behind the studio or Founder POV every other week. Check `tracker.csv` before picking a topic so pillars and angles don't repeat back to back.

Status: **draft**. Only "Honest advice" was set by Ramit. The other four are proposed from the site's services and case studies. Confirm, rename or cut them.

## 1. Honest advice
The straight answer agencies don't give. What actually moves sales, what's a waste of money, and when not to hire us.
- Example topics: why beautiful stores still underperform (Brief 1), too many apps, when a theme is enough, redesign vs. fix, what an audit usually finds.
- Best formats: LinkedIn post, carousel, article.

## 2. Proof
Real client work, with results quoted exactly from `voice.md`.
- Example topics: how Halm got to 400+ reviews on subscription, Naba's path to acquisition, Just Good Soil's lab integration, Sapori's brand.
- Best formats: carousel (before/after, the build), article as case-study summary, LinkedIn post with one result.

## 3. Shopify know-how
Practical, specific how-tos that show depth.
- Example topics: migrating without losing SEO, checkout steps to cut, speed fixes, subscriptions, B2B on Shopify, getting found in ChatGPT and AI answers.
- Best formats: article (SEO), carousel checklists, newsletter.

## 4. Behind the studio
How we work: the $99 video audit, fixed prices, senior people only, no ticket queue.
- Example topics: what's inside a video audit, why we give fixed prices, a week of monthly care, how we built Ovi.
- Best formats: LinkedIn video, LinkedIn post, Facebook.

## 5. Founder POV
Ramit's opinions and lessons, written in first person for his personal LinkedIn.
- Example topics: ten years on Shopify, mistakes we made, what AI changes for small stores, saying no to projects.
- Best formats: LinkedIn post (personal), video.

## Audiences
- **New founders**: first store, unsure what they need.
- **Movers**: on WooCommerce, Squarespace or Wix, considering Shopify.
- **Growing stores**: live on Shopify, traffic but weak sales.
- **Everyone**: a mixed audience, so write for growing stores.
